## H1: APAAR consent and UDISE+ preparation, described honestly

APAAR is voluntary and needs a parent's consent. Unifloe records that consent on its own, keeps the identity records a school needs for UDISE+, and stores no Aadhaar number.

## Separate consent

APAAR generation has its own consent type. A parent can decline it and still use Unifloe.

## No Aadhaar stored

No Aadhaar number anywhere, and never in a log, an export or a link.

## Exports, not integrations

UDISE+ returns come from the school's own extracts. No live government connection.

## Consent that is recorded on its own

Every consent record carries a type. Platform terms and data processing are needed to sign in; APAAR generation is a third, separate type.

- Three consent types, recorded independently
- Verification method and notice version on every record
- Withdrawal recorded, never silently deleted
- Full consent history per student
## What Unifloe keeps for UDISE+

Every recognised school files annual data into UDISE+ keyed to the Permanent Education Number.

- Roster, class placement and attendance history
- Guardian links and consent
- Exports the school downloads, checks and submits
- No live connection to a government system
## Use readiness language accurately

Unifloe helps a school organise records and consent around APAAR. It does not issue APAAR IDs, certify a school, guarantee acceptance or replace official guidance.

- Hold a masked reference and a verification outcome, never the Aadhaar number
- Verify current requirements before regulatory decisions
- Take appropriate professional or government guidance
Unifloe is not APAAR certified, government approved or affiliated with the Government of India. This page describes product behaviour, not legal or regulatory advice.
