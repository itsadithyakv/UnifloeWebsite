## Questions schools ask.

Straight answers on GST, limits, billing and leaving.

### Is GST included?

Prices are shown before GST. Where GST applies it is added on the invoice at the applicable rate.

### Who counts as a user on the Free plan?

Everyone who signs in: students, teachers, admin staff and parents. The Free plan covers up to 100 of them for one class.

### What happens when we pass the student limit?

You move to the next plan. PaperKite tells you before it happens, and nothing is switched off in the meantime.

### Can we pay monthly and change later?

Yes. A monthly plan runs to the end of the paid month. A yearly plan runs to the end of the year and works out to two months free.

### What if we stop?

Your school's data is exported for you before the account is closed. Closing a school is staged and reversible until the final step.

### How do we pay?

PaperKite invoices the school directly, monthly or yearly, and records the payment. There is no online checkout and no card details are stored.
