## H1: Start using Unifloe in a few clear steps.

From the live demo to the first parent signing in. Free for one class, and PaperKite does the first steps with you.

## Look first

Open the live demo as a principal, teacher, parent, librarian or warden. It has a guided tour, and changes stay in your browser for 24 hours.

## Start free

One class, up to 100 users, at no cost for as long as you like. Tell us the school, we call back within a working day, and we set it up with you.

## Already on Unifloe

Staff, students and parents sign in with the school code. The same address installs as an app on a phone or laptop.

## What to have ready

- One person to hold the Head Admin account
- Your admission register or roster as a spreadsheet
- A phone number and email for each parent you want to activate
- Your logo as a PNG, JPEG or WebP
- The classes and sections you run this year
- Your fee structure for the year, if fees are in scope
## Settled before go live

These are agreed between PaperKite and the school, because the school is the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act and PaperKite is its processor.

- A data processing agreement between PaperKite and the school
- The privacy notice your school publishes to parents
- A named grievance officer on the school side
- How parents give consent, decided against your own admission form
## Pick the board format first

The format decides the vocabulary, the class bands, the assessment modes and the report card template. Everything else follows from it.

### CBSE

Nursery to Class 12 across the foundational, preparatory, middle and secondary stages.

### ICSE and ISC

Groups I to III, SUPW and the senior secondary streams.

### State board

The same school shape with the school's own class bands and terms.

### Karnataka PU

I PUC and II PUC as a college, with lecturers, class in charges and the subject combinations the college creates.
