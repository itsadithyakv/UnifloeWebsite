## H1: School ERP software built for Indian school operations

Unifloe connects academic, administrative, financial, communication and campus work for CBSE, ICSE, state board and Karnataka PU institutions.

## ERP and LMS together

Attendance, marks, fees and requests share the same classes, people and permissions as assignments.

## Built for Indian schools

Board formats, the 75 percent attendance threshold, hall tickets, receipts in rupees and DPDP consent.

## Configurable by school

Logo, colours, terminology, enabled modules and academic structure adapt to each institution.

## Replace fragmented school operations with connected workflows

Attendance, fees, assignments, requests and announcements depend on the same students, families, staff and classes. Unifloe keeps them in one role aware system.

- Classes, sections, subjects, timetables and attendance
- Assignments, materials, quizzes and feedback
- Fees, dues, receipts and concessions
- Announcements, requests and approvals
## Start from school structure and responsibility

Each school has its own separate space, and authority comes from assignments rather than broad labels.

- Own branding, modules, users and data boundary
- Server side checks even when the screen already hid an action
- Class teacher, subject and ownership authority from assignments
- Leadership sees whole school exceptions; families see only their own records
## Roll out the workflows your school is ready to adopt

A school does not need every module on day one.

- The basics cover the teaching day, communication, finance, people and administration
- Library, hostel, front office and inventory switch on when the school needs them
- Scope and onboarding milestones agreed before rollout
