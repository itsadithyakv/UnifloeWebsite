## What schools tell us.

In conversations with Bengaluru schools the same problems come up again and again. Here is what we hear, and what Unifloe does about each one.

- A schoolAttendance is on paper, then typed up, and parents hear about an absence days later.UnifloeTeachers mark the register on a phone. A parent sees the absence the same day.
- A schoolReport cards are built in spreadsheets every term, by hand, with mistakes.UnifloeMarks go in once. Report cards and hall tickets print from them in the school's own format.
- A schoolFee receipts, concessions and dues live in three different registers.UnifloeOne ledger. A payment recorded at the counter updates dues and issues the receipt in one step.
- A schoolUDISE+, APAAR consent and the DPDP Act are a pile of paper nobody owns.UnifloeParent consent is recorded once at sign in, and the records UDISE+ needs export in one go.
