## Why we built Unifloe

In conversations with Bengaluru schools the same things came up: attendance on paper typed up later, report cards rebuilt in spreadsheets every term, fees in three registers, and consent paperwork nobody owned.

- Mark attendance once, on a phone, and let parents see it
- Enter marks once and print report cards and hall tickets from them
- One fee ledger, with receipts issued the moment a payment is recorded
- Parent consent recorded properly under the DPDP Act

## How we work with a school

You look first, we call back, and we do the setup with you.

- Try the live demo with its guided tour, no account needed
- Send four fields and we call back within a working day
- We create the school and hand over the Head Admin login
- A review point after the first term, so the rollout stays calm
## What we will not do

Schools hold children's data, so a few things are fixed.

- No advertising, no analytics that track children, no selling of data
- No card or bank details stored, and no Aadhaar numbers
- No consent given on a parent's behalf
- No feature described on this site that does not exist; what is coming is labelled coming soon
